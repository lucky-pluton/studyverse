Buildings, roads, water and green areas come from OpenStreetMap (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL). Heights use the mapped floor count where there is one; the rest are estimated from building type and size. Unnamed buildings are labelled by what their map tags say they are and the nearest named place or road. Trees, deer and traffic are illustrative.

The seal on the Main Gate sign is the IIT Madras logo by Indian Institute of Technology Madras, from Wikimedia Commons, licensed CC BY-SA 4.0 and recoloured gold here. Sahej-Shala is not affiliated with or endorsed by IIT Madras.

Satellite view: imagery powered by Esri (source: Esri, Maxar, Earthstar Geographics, and the GIS User Community), loaded in your browser while you view the page and draped over the ground and roofs. It was also used as a reference to place the Open Air Theatre and Gajendra Circle.
